Know more about us


Zomato is a restaurant searching platform and discovery service founded in 2008. Service provided by zomato i.e restaurant searching, discovery, online ordering and table reservation etc. Food delivery apps have become a part of our everyday lives, and among them, Zomato stands out as one of the most popular. It helps users explore restaurants and order food easily.
Better food for more people. Through innovative technology, deep local insights, and a relentless focus on customer experience, Zomato is reshaping the food landscape one meal at a time.
